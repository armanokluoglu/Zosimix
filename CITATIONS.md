# Data and citations

Zosimix does not redistribute raw dataset audio. The repo contains download scripts and task definitions. Any audio that *is* published, such as demo clips or modified stems, follows the dataset's license and carries the attribution below.

**Required:** any write-up, blog post, paper or video that uses results or audio from these datasets must cite them as shown here.
